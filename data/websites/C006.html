About us

Bergmann Fertigungs-IT GmbH is based in DE and works in industrial software. ERP add-ons for discrete manufacturing; flat growth.

The company employs about 260 people and is owned by its private equity shareholders.

Products

Our products help manufacturers collect machine data, plan production and reduce downtime.

Customers

We serve industrial customers across Europe, mainly in automotive, food, pharma and machine building.

News

Revenue growth was flat for the second year. The company is preparing a cloud version of its ERP add-ons to restart growth.
