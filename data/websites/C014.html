About us

Bodensee Vision GmbH is based in DE and works in automation. Machine-vision quality inspection; 62% of revenue comes from one automotive customer (customer concentration).

The company employs about 105 people and is owned by its vc-backed shareholders.

Products

Our products help manufacturers collect machine data, plan production and reduce downtime.

Customers

We serve industrial customers across Europe, mainly in automotive, food, pharma and machine building.

News

Bodensee Vision renewed its framework contract with its largest automotive customer until 2029. This single customer accounts for 62% of annual revenue.

The company plans to win two new customers in electronics manufacturing to reduce this dependency.
