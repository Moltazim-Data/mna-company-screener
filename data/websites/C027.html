About us

Frankfurt Fabrikdaten AG is based in DE and works in industrial software. OEE analytics; company is under sanctions review due to a Russian shareholder.

The company employs about 190 people and is owned by its founder shareholders.

Products

Our products help manufacturers collect machine data, plan production and reduce downtime.

Customers

We serve industrial customers across Europe, mainly in automotive, food, pharma and machine building.

News

German authorities opened a sanctions review of Frankfurt Fabrikdaten because one minority shareholder is a holding company registered in Russia. Payments to this shareholder are currently frozen.

Until the review is closed, the company cannot sign new public-sector contracts.
