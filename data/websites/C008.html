About us

Tirol IoT Solutions GmbH is based in AT and works in iot. Smart-building sensors; ongoing litigation with a former distributor over patent rights.

The company employs about 90 people and is owned by its founder shareholders.

Products

Our products help manufacturers collect machine data, plan production and reduce downtime.

Customers

We serve industrial customers across Europe, mainly in automotive, food, pharma and machine building.

News

A former distributor filed a patent infringement lawsuit against Tirol IoT Solutions in 2026. The claim covers the core sensor technology, and the first court hearing is scheduled for spring 2027.

Management expects the legal dispute to take at least two years.
