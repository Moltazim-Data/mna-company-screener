About us

Hamburg Supply Chain IT GmbH is based in DE and works in industrial software. Warehouse management software; restructuring completed in 2024, now profitable.

The company employs about 175 people and is owned by its founder shareholders.

Products

Our products help manufacturers collect machine data, plan production and reduce downtime.

Customers

We serve industrial customers across Europe, mainly in automotive, food, pharma and machine building.

News

Hamburg Supply Chain IT completed its restructuring programme in 2024. Two loss-making units were sold, and the company has been profitable every quarter since Q3 2024.

A new CFO joined in 2025 and the bank loans were refinanced on normal terms.
